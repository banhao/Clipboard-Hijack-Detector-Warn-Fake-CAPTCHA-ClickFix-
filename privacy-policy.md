# Privacy Policy for [ClickFix & URL Hijack Shield]
Effective Date: June 24, 2026

## 1. Overview
We respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle and protect data in relation to your use of the [ClickFix & URL Hijack Shield] Google Chrome extension.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
This extension operates entirely locally within your browser. 
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, gather, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, or browsing history).
- **Permissions Utilization:** The extension utilizes permissions such as `clipboardRead` and host permissions solely to analyze data locally in real-time to detect threat indicators (like malicious clipboard hijacking injections and fake CAPTCHAs). This data is processed in-memory and is never saved or transmitted off your device.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
Because no data is ever collected or transmitted, absolutely zero user data is shared with, transferred to, or sold to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

## 4. Security
We are committed to securing your environment. All features perform local analysis using modern security practices inside the browser sandbox.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected immediately by an updated "Effective Date" above.
